QuiverLog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

QuiverLog is built around a simple principle: your data is yours and it stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. QuiverLog does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs and no server — the app works fully offline.

Where your scores live

All scoring sessions, equipment details and sight marks are stored locally on your device only. If you choose to export a backup, the file is created on your device and shared only where you send it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

QuiverLog does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
